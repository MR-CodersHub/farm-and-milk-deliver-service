Read our sourcing standards

From a named farm, not a map

Each batch of Sunrise Whole Milk is collected from a single partner farm — usually Kannan's pasture in Anaikatti. The herd grazes on rotated grass and is never given growth hormones. We collect at 4:30 AM, chill to 4°C before 5:30, and gently pasteurise at 69°C. The cream line will rise overnight because we don't homogenise. Shake gently, don't stir hard.

What we make with it at home

  • Warm gently for a bedtime glass with honey and nutmeg.

  • Set overnight with a spoonful of curd for a fresh bowl at breakfast.
